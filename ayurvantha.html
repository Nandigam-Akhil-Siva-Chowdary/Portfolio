Applications

One ecosystem for every healthcare touchpoint

Each app handles a specific role while sharing the information needed to reduce delays, improve coordination, and support better medical decisions.

Patient Application

  • Multi-language support for accessible healthcare usage.
  • Unique Ayur ID generated from Aadhaar details to keep medical records in one place.
  • Online consultations, hospital visit booking, payments, and medical history access.
  • Smart hospital recommendations using condition, ratings, and reviews.
  • AI virtual doctor for temporary relief tips and diet suggestions before consultation.

Hospital Application

  • Role-based workflow for doctors, receptionists, reportists, and admins.
  • Receptionists receive patient requests and assign cases to suitable doctors.
  • Doctors request tests, reportists upload reports, and prescriptions are issued digitally.
  • Billing covers consultations, tests, prescriptions, and payment processing.
  • Admin tools manage staff, hospital details, medicines, home tests, equipment, diet plans, and medication reminders.

Doctor Application

  • Independent doctors can provide appointment-based online consultations.
  • Supports video calls, voice calls, and messaging for minor health issues.
  • Patients can request a follow-up consultation at no additional cost when an issue remains unresolved.

Pharmacy Application

  • Partner pharmacies can list medicines and medical equipment.
  • Nearby pharmacies are notified when patients place orders.
  • The pharmacy with available stock accepts the order and shares pickup details with delivery partners.

Delivery Application

  • Delivery partners transport medicines and medical equipment.
  • Home visits can support basic health checkups when booked by patients.
  • Orders include both pharmacy pickup and patient delivery address details.

Ambulance Application

  • Patient location and Ayur ID are instantly shared with ambulance drivers.
  • Drivers receive direct navigation to the patient.
  • Traffic police alerts help clear routes and reduce emergency response time.
