Problem & solution

Early detection for safer pregnancies

Maternal mortality remains a major global challenge, with preeclampsia as one of the leading causes. MaatriRaksha brings continuous monitoring, offline AI, and emergency response into a single wearable-mobile solution.

Problem statement

  • Pregnancy hypertension and proteinuria are diagnosed too late for many women.
  • Periodic visits miss sudden blood pressure spikes and rising risk.
  • Rural and low-resource communities lack continuous access to healthcare.
  • Delayed diagnosis increases maternal and fetal risks significantly.

Our solution

  • ESP32-based wristband captures blood flow, motion, and oxygen signals every five minutes.
  • Bluetooth Low Energy sends validated readings to a Flutter smartphone app.
  • On-device TensorFlow Lite model estimates systolic and diastolic blood pressure offline.
  • Automated alerts recommend urine testing or emergency contact notification when risk is high.
System workflow

How MaatriRaksha works

Workflow steps

1

The wristband collects PPG, motion, and SpO₂ data every five minutes, removing artifacts using accelerometer signals.

2

Validated readings transmit over BLE to the phone and save locally in SQLite for offline reliability.

3

A TensorFlow Lite model estimates systolic and diastolic blood pressure directly on the smartphone.

4

Elevated BP alert triggers if readings exceed 140/90 for two consecutive measurements.

5

The app guides the user to capture a urine dipstick image for AI-based protein level analysis.

6

The computer vision model classifies protein as negative, trace, or 1+ through 4+.

7

MaatriRaksha combines blood pressure, trend, gestational age, and protein result into a risk prediction.

8

Likely preeclampsia triggers SMS and WhatsApp alerts to emergency contacts, including location and critical readings.

Key innovations
